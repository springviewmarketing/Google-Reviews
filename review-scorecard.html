Where do I find this?

The easy route, on a computer rather than a phone.

  1. Go to google.com and sign in with the email address that manages your practice listing.
  2. Search for your practice name. Your own listing appears with an editing panel.
  3. Click Read Reviews, then Get more reviews.
  4. Copy the link it shows you and paste it into the box above.

If you cannot see that panel, search Google for "Place ID Finder", find your practice on the map it shows, and copy the Place ID. It starts with ChIJ. Paste that in the box above instead and this page will build the full link for you.

A link that starts maps.app.goo.gl is a sharing link, not a review link. It will not work here. Use one of the two routes above.

What is this and how do I get one?

A short link is a tidy web address on your own website that automatically forwards to the long Google one. The patient types nine words instead of ninety characters.

Whoever looks after your website can set it up in about five minutes. The thing to ask for is a redirect from /review to your Google review link. Wix, Squarespace and WordPress all have this built in under a settings menu called Redirects or URL Redirects.

You do not need one to use this page. Everything works with the Google link. A short link just makes your text messages one message instead of two, and makes the printed card readable by someone without their glasses on.
